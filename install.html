Private beta

Install Cogdex on your Mac

One app. Nothing is uploaded. The first launch downloads the private engine (a few gigabytes, once). After that it works with Wi-Fi off.

  1. 1

    Download

    Apple silicon only (M1 or newer), macOS 12 or later, 16 GB of memory.

    Download Cogdex.dmg

  2. 2

    Drag to Applications

    Open the disk image and drag Cogdex into the Applications folder.

  3. 3

    Allow it once

    macOS will warn that Apple cannot verify Cogdex. That is expected for this beta: the app is not yet signed with an Apple developer certificate.

Open Anyway

The one-time Gatekeeper step

  1. 1

    Open Cogdex

    From Applications, double-click Cogdex. A warning appears. Click Done or OK — do not move it to the Trash.

  2. 2

    Open Privacy & Security

    Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to the Security section.

  3. 3

    Click Open Anyway

    Next to “Cogdex was blocked”, click Open Anyway, then confirm. You only do this once. After that, Cogdex opens like any other app.

If you do not see “Open Anyway”, Control-click Cogdex in Applications, choose Open, then click Open in the dialog.

Then

Stay on Setup until it says Ready

First launch downloads the private engine and the assistant. Keep the Mac awake and online. When Setup says Ready, drop files into your Cogdex folder and ask a question.
