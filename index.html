What it does

Answers from your own files.
Not from the internet.

Reads scans and photos

Snap a picture of an ID card or drop in a scanned PDF. Cogdex reads the text, including the machine-readable lines at the bottom of a passport.

Does the date math

Ask when something expires, how old someone is, or when you last travelled. Dates are worked out exactly, not guessed.

Clear, tidy answers

A single fact comes back as one sentence. Lists, like trips or renewals, come back as a neat table you can scan at a glance.

Asks when it's unsure

"Insurance number" could mean health or car. Cogdex asks which one you mean instead of picking the wrong card.

Shows its source

Every answer names the file it came from, so you can check it. If the answer isn't in your files, it says so rather than making one up.

Private by design

Offline means offline.

Most AI assistants send your questions to a data centre. Cogdex can't: the AI model, the search index and your documents all live on your Mac. After a one-time setup download, you can turn the Wi-Fi off and it keeps working.

A local AI model

An open Llama model runs on your Mac's own chip. No API keys and no cloud calls.

An index on your disk

Documents are split into passages and embedded into a SQLite file in your Library folder.

Hybrid search

Meaning-based and keyword search are blended, so exact numbers and loose wording both land.

Yours to delete

Clear the index or a single chat any time. Deleting the app's folder removes everything.

Questions

Good to know.

Does it need the internet?

Only once, to download the AI model during setup. After that Cogdex works completely offline, and your questions and documents never leave your Mac.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS with 16 GB of memory and about 5 GB of free space. Apple silicon (M1 or newer) is recommended for fast answers.

Can it get things wrong?

It can, as any AI can. That's why every answer shows the file it came from, dates are calculated exactly rather than guessed, and Cogdex says "I don't know" when the answer isn't in your files.

Where are my documents stored?

Your files stay in your Cogdex folder. The index Cogdex builds from them is a single file on your Mac. Nothing is copied to a server, because there is no server.
