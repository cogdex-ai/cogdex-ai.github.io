Nothing leaves your Mac

Your documents, questions and answers stay on your computer. No account, no cloud, no uploads. After setup, it works without the internet.

Reads scans and photos

Snap a picture of an ID card or drop in a scanned PDF. Cogdex reads the text, including the machine-readable lines on passports.

Understands dates

Ask when something expires, how old someone is, or what's coming up. Cogdex does the date math for you, so you don't have to.

Says “I don't know”

If the answer isn't in your files, Cogdex tells you instead of guessing. When it does answer, it shows which file the answer came from.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install Cogdex

    One app. On first launch it sets itself up, a one-time download of a few gigabytes.

  2. 2

    Add your files

    Drop documents, PDFs and photos into your Cogdex folder. Cogdex reads them automatically.

  3. 3

    Ask anything

    “Which of our passports expire this year?” “What's my blood type?” Answers in seconds.
